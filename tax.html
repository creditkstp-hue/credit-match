Where tax preparation fits your goals

Small-business owners

Keep business records separate, maintain bookkeeping, track business expenses and understand the returns and filings that may apply to your business entity.

Tax readiness checklist

1. Know your filing situation

Requirements vary by filing status, income, business structure, and other circumstances. Not everyone with income necessarily owes income tax or must file.

2. Organize your records

Gather relevant income forms and expense summaries. Businesses should keep transaction records and separate personal from business costs.

3. Review tax payments and potential credits

Self-employed people may need estimated tax payments. A tax professional can help assess business and personal filing considerations.

4. Decide between free filing and professional help

Use free options if suitable. Compare services, costs, credentials and privacy standards when hiring a paid preparer.
