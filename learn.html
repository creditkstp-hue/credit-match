SBA business credit guidance ↗

Your business credit checklist

01

Set up your business correctly

Choose an appropriate structure, register with your state/local agencies as required, and keep your legal business name and address consistent. An LLC isn't mandatory for all business credit, and formation by itself won't create credit.

02

Get an EIN if applicable

Apply free through the IRS. Never pay a third party just to obtain a free EIN. Some sole proprietors may use an SSN instead.

03

Separate your business finances

Open a dedicated business bank account when appropriate, maintain accurate records, and avoid routinely mixing personal and business spending.

04

Check your business credit files

Review information held by business credit reporting companies such as Dun & Bradstreet, Experian Business, and Equifax Business. Products and access options vary; check each official website.

05

Use vendors that really report

Only open useful accounts after confirming exactly which business bureau the vendor reports to, what activity it reports, its costs, and when reporting begins. Many vendors do not report.

06

Pay on time and monitor progress

Pay every obligation by its due date, keep balances manageable, and verify that the expected trade history appears. Be cautious about financing offers that require a personal guarantee.

Business credit questions people ask

Can I build business credit with an LLC and EIN?

They can be part of the setup, but they don't automatically create credit history. You generally need financial accounts or trade relationships that report qualifying activity to business reporting agencies.

What are net-30 vendor accounts?

These are purchasing terms that commonly allow payment within 30 days. Only a vendor that actually reports positive payment history to a relevant business bureau can help establish that reported history. Don't buy products you don't need just to obtain a tradeline.

Can I get business credit without a personal guarantee?

Some products may not require one, but many lenders and business card issuers review the owner's personal credit or ask for a personal guarantee. Requirements depend on the institution, business performance, and product.

Does my business need to make money first?

Revenue is not universally required to establish a business credit file, but many funding products assess revenue, bank deposits, operating history, and repayment ability. New businesses should focus first on building a sound foundation.

How long until I have good business credit?

There is no reliable universal timeline. It depends on verified reporting, account history, payment behavior, and the score model. Avoid anyone promising an immediate score or guaranteed funding.

Your personal credit checklist

01

Review your credit reports

Read the account, balance, inquiry, and payment information from the major consumer credit bureaus. A credit report is not the same as a credit score.

02

Catch up on payments

Prioritize getting due accounts current and paying future bills on time. Use reminders or autopay only when you can cover the payment.

03

Manage credit card balances

Lower utilization where practical without missing essential bills. There is no single balance threshold that guarantees a score increase.

04

Correct reporting mistakes

Dispute inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information with the reporting bureau and the information provider. You can dispute errors for free; accurate negative information generally cannot be removed just because it hurts your score.

05

Consider credit-building products carefully

If you lack positive history, compare secured cards or credit-builder products for fees, reporting, repayment terms, and total costs. Don't open debt you cannot afford.

06

Monitor and avoid unnecessary applications

Check progress periodically and only apply for credit you need. Repeated applications over a short period may affect some credit scores.

Personal credit questions people ask

What's the fastest way to build credit?

There is no guaranteed shortcut. Paying on time, lowering high revolving balances, correcting errors, and building a sustained positive history are generally more dependable than purchasing tradelines.

Can accurate collections or late payments be removed?

Usually not simply because they are negative. Dispute items that are inaccurate or incomplete; seek help from a qualified professional when you need case-specific advice.

Is a secured credit card worth considering?

It may help establish history if the issuer reports payments and you use it responsibly. Compare refundable deposits, annual fees, APR, and reporting policies.

Do I have to pay to see my credit reports?

No. Free consumer credit reports are available through AnnualCreditReport.com. Certain credit score services may be separate.
