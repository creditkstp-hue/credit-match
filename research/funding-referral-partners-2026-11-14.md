## Legal and operating safeguards
- Credit Match will operate as an **education and referral publisher initially**, not promise to broker or approve loans. If it starts negotiating, communicating financing offers, collecting compensation tied to commercial financing, or servicing loans, obtain jurisdiction-specific advice on licensing, disclosures, contracts and fee restrictions *before* launch.
- Georgia's commercial financing law, O.C.G.A. §10-1-393.18, defines certain commission-motivated brokers, requires disclosures on covered commercial transactions, and prohibits commercial financing brokers from charging an advance fee to a business for brokering services, subject to listed exceptions. See https://georgiacommons.org/code/10-1-393.18
- Consumer lending referrals can trigger state licensing, marketing, fair-lending and privacy rules. Obtain advice for consumer loan referrals in each target state. Refer applications to approved partners rather than collecting SSNs, bank logins or documents.
- FTC affiliate disclosure guidance says disclosures should be clear and conspicuous and proximate to affiliate recommendations: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking
- Never promise loan approval, funding amounts, rates or timelines. Don't call editorial recommendations underwriting or prequalification.
- Contract checklist: approved acquisition channels; lead sourcing and ownership; eligible states; broker/licensing obligations; actual tracking URL or CRM introduction; payout event; payout rate and timing; clawbacks; privacy/data sharing; consumer disclosures; whether redirect URLs are allowed; required branded copy.
- For now, `/funding.html` is a no-login, no-PII browser-only readiness guide with partner-inquiry resources. Existing affiliate ledger could manually record verified commissions once contracted, but it does not yet attribute funded deals automatically.
