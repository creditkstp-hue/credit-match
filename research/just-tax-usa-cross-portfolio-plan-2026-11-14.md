# Just Tax USA — Cross-Portfolio Recommendation Plan
Date: November 14, 2026
Status: strategic rollout approved; Credit Match informational page and contextual links committed to its development branch; other brands not yet modified.

## Positioning
Just Tax USA is the portfolio's shared **tax-preparation referral resource**, not a competing top-level software build. Visitors should be offered tax education and an optional connection to the tax team when relevant. Do not falsely imply every earner has taxable income, must file, owes tax, or qualifies for an advance. Avoid making claims of CPA, enrolled-agent, or attorney qualifications without checking each preparer's actual credentials. Confirm current PTIN credentials for paid federal return preparers.

## Suggested reusable recommendation copy
**For business owners:** "Running a business also means keeping your tax records organized. Explore Just Tax USA's tax-readiness resources and free IRS filing information."
**For self-employed people:** "Self-employed or earning side income? Review your filing requirements and explore tax preparation options with Just Tax USA, if professional help would be useful."
**For individuals:** "Need help preparing your return? Compare free filing options and paid preparers. Just Tax USA is one related option; review qualifications and service fees."
**For contractors:** "Independent contractors may have self-employment tax and estimated payment obligations. Review IRS resources; you may choose any qualified tax preparer."

## Before activating tax preparation lead intake
1. Verify current official Just Tax USA website, business email, booking/contact link and services offered, along with actual team credentials.
2. Confirm whether Just Tax USA itself earns service fees, commission, or referral compensation from a preparation partner and document payment treatment.
3. Confirm opt-in wording and obtain permission before sharing any lead information between Credit Match / STS / PHB / Trivo and Just Tax USA.
4. Build a secure tax client portal through the tax provider if handling returns; never request SSNs or tax documents in the general credit intake.
5. Include clear relationship disclosures, free IRS options, and appropriate no-guarantee statements; don't advertise unverified same-day advances.
6. Track referrals at source/brand level without assuming a referral counts as a paid filing; measure **completed paid tax preparations**, not only clicks.
