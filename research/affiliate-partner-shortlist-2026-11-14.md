## Prospect onboarding checklist
1. Record program application URL, brand, category, geographic restrictions and approval status.
2. Confirm acquisition channel permissions: questionnaire matching, comparison results, email followups, social media and link redirects.
3. Review all product fees, APRs, withdrawal/cancellation terms, claims and restrictions; do not imply approval.
4. Obtain the **actual assigned tracking URL**, payout conditions, cookie window, reversal/clawback policy, payment threshold and frequency.
5. Capture approval date, program contact, legal documents and mandatory copy/disclosures.
6. Create the offer as **inactive** in the dashboard. Test tracking, then activate after approval.
7. Check partner terms for redirect-based click tracking; some affiliate programs prohibit intermediating or modifying links.
8. Store partner conversion IDs and verified approved/payout status when reporting integration is built. Do not treat clicks as earned commissions.
9. Avoid recommending unnecessary paid services. Free weekly credit reports are available from AnnualCreditReport.com.

### Matching gaps to develop
- Credit-building products: payment history/thin file, avoid guaranteed improvements
- Secured credit cards: deposit, fees and issuer eligibility
- Personal loan comparison: APR, fees, ability to repay, qualified application flow
- Debt consolidation: assess total cost and alternative options
- Business credit: business age, revenues, deposits and commercial product fit

### Advertising compliance
FTC affiliate relationships must be disclosed clearly and close to each offer. Do not rank paid offers purely by commission under a false suitability label.
FTC guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking
